I am very proud to share that I have received the **Best PhD Thesis in Artificial Intelligence 2025 Award**, granted by the [**Portuguese Association for Artificial Intelligence (APPIA)**](https://www.appia.pt/)! 🏆🎉

This award recognises **PhD theses in Artificial Intelligence** completed at Portuguese higher-education institutions during 2025.

I am very glad since this award recognises the research carried out throughout my PhD and its contributions at the intersection of **Artificial Intelligence, Software Engineering, and Computer-aided Education**.

My doctoral research led to [**MENTOR**](/projects/mentor/), a framework focused on advancing automated feedback and program repair for programming education. This work was developed in collaboration with my supervisors **Vasco Manquinho** at [INESC-ID](https://inesc-id.pt) / [Técnico U. Lisboa](https://tecnico.ulisboa.pt/en/) and **Mikoláš Janota** at [CIIRC](https://www.ciirc.cvut.cz), Czech Technical University in Prague.

I am very grateful to APPIA and the award jury, as well as to my supervisors, colleagues, and everyone who supported me along the way.
